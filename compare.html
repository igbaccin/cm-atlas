Fixed territory colours

Holes are missing annual figures, never zeroes. Ticks on a territory's line mark its observed changes of list; hover for both lists and the lens reading. Pale segments and points mark a combined source item. A three-year mean requires three consecutive observed years. Zeroes remain in the table when a logarithmic axis cannot draw them.

The ten leading groups, year by year

Rank 1 is the largest printed figure. Only groups observed in a year's ten leading groups are drawn; a missing year or a year outside the top ten breaks the line. Ties use classification order. Labels name each group's first and last visible rank. Ranks use unsmoothed annual figures. Unknown / uncoded lines follow the shared switch. A combined historical item retains its lens label.
