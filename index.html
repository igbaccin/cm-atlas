Reading the returns, year by year

Eleven territories, 1887–1967. Begin with what survives: patient streams, gaps and the lists used to name disease.

The source landscape

Each square is a report year. Its horizontal stripes show the patient streams present; hatching marks returns with totals and no disease lines. The thin band underneath records the list in force, with a tick at each observed change. Hover for the streams, list and coded-line count; select a square to read that territory and year. Empty squares are absent returns, never zero cases.
