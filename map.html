The selected indicator

Bins are fixed across all years, 1887–1967, for the current indicator, stream, population, measure and scale. Decades are arithmetic means of available annual values, with each territory's number of years in the tooltip. The Map shows all eleven territories; the shared territory selection carries to Compare. Maps use unsmoothed annual values; logarithmic axes and smoothing apply to series charts. Unknown / uncoded lines are excluded from these coded indicators.

Boundaries and names

Boundaries are modern, from world-atlas 2.0.2 (Natural Earth). Northern Rhodesia is shown as Zambia; Bechuanaland as Botswana; Belgian Congo as the Democratic Republic of the Congo; Gold Coast as Ghana; Nyasaland as Malawi; Tanganyika as mainland Tanzania; and Zanzibar as Unguja and Pemba. Uganda, Sierra Leone, Kenya and Nigeria use their modern boundaries. This locates the returns and does not reconstruct colonial coverage. Select a territory to open its figures and printed sources. In a decade view, this opens the latest available indicator year in that decade, or its first year where none is available.
