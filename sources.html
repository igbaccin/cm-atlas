In-patient arithmetic

Start of year plus admissions should equal treated. Pass and fail counts refer to flagged printed figures; all measures attached to a checked triple carry the flag. Unchecked includes other line figures. No value is repaired.

Repeated publications excluded

source_repeat = 1

N15 flags the blocks and duplicate source addresses identified in N06. The atlas excludes them by default. The shared switch can display the repeated-publication overlay; it can include the same patients again.

How a printed figure is counted once

Use a printed territory total instead of its places, both sexes instead of sex margins, annual figures instead of subannual parts, and all institutions instead of their kinds. Otherwise sum non-overlapping parts. Keep each printed population, and provide a separate all-population margin, marked summed where no total exists. No total is added to its parts. Numeric source cells alone contribute; unknown codes remain visible.

The lenses and their approximations

Holding items, report-as combinations and approximation notes are carried from the supplied lens tables. A finer historical reading is combined where its source item cannot distinguish the target items. Local and unidentified lists keep an explicit lens gap.
